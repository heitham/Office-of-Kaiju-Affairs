Apply — Proximity Construction Permit

Required before building, extending or structurally altering any structure within 1,000 metres of a designated kaiju corridor centreline. Zone 4 sites require reinforcement standard K-2; Zone 5 sites require K-3. The fee of ¥45,000 is payable on submission.
