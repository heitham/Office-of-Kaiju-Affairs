Invented traces used to build the Arena before any runs had been recorded.
**They are not indexed in `../index.json` and are never rendered.**

Keep them only as provenance. Their numbers are the *inverse* of what was
actually measured: these files show the UI-guessing agent failing and, in
`ui-task-3-permit.json`, submitting the permit form. Neither happened. In the
recorded runs that lane scores at or near 1.00 and never pressed Submit.

Nothing in here may be re-indexed. The Arena renders recorded runs or it
renders nothing.
